Everything a group needs to get work done and prove it

Built for classrooms and teams. Simple enough to start today.

Assign tasks with a real deadline

Give a task a title, description, a due date and exact time, and reference links. Assign it to a group and everyone in it gets it.

Deadlines that mean it

Once the due time passes, an unsubmitted task shows “Deadline Passed” and can no longer be handed in.

Submit with text and links

Members write a response and attach links, such as a doc, a repo or a video. If a task is rejected, they can submit again before the deadline.

Groups inside groups

Organize by school, class, department or team. Nest sub-groups, add existing accounts, or remove people whenever things change.

Performance you can read at a glance

See each person’s history as on-time and missed tasks. Switch between 1D, 5D, 1W, 1M, 6M, 1Y and ALL, and download it as Excel. Members get their own 60-day activity graph.

Accounts you control

Create accounts under your organization, with a random or custom password. Re-roll a password or delete an account when needed. Members only see what is assigned to them.

Notes that keep up with you

Write with bold, italic, headings, lists, quotes, links and code. Notes autosave, show word count and read time, and sync across your devices.

Keyboard first

Press Ctrl K for the command palette, Ctrl J to switch light and dark.

Light on any device

Notes are stored on your device first and queued for sync, so writing never waits on a connection. Time12 runs on phones, school laptops and older hardware.
